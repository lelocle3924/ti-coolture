What moving search costs, and how to cover it. SRS §5.1 puts a live search bar on the homepage as HP-02, P0. The feature is unchanged — only its position — but two things follow.

First, an icon is less discoverable than a field. That mostly affects visitors who arrive knowing what they want, which is a smaller group than P1, so the trade is reasonable. It does slightly reduce the volume of search_logs and zero-result data, and §8.3 group C calls that the gold mine — worth watching in the first fortnight rather than assuming it's fine.

Second, an icon-only control needs an accessible name (aria-label="Tìm kiếm") and a real 44px target, which at a 22px glyph means 11px of padding on each side. On desktop the header already carries a full field, so this only applies to mobile. HP-02 §6.4 §8.3
2
Hero spec — needs numbers before you brief the shops. Three things to fix now, because re-collecting images from shops later is expensive. (a) One canonical ratio. Portrait 4:5 works on both: mobile shows it whole, desktop centre-crops to a wide band. Two crops per submission would be more control but doubles the ask on every shop. (b) A safe zone. Nothing meaningful within ~15% of the top or bottom edge, or desktop cropping will decapitate it. (c) A quality floor — suggest ≥1600×2000, sRGB, no text baked into the image (it can't be translated for the EN build, and it won't survive cropping).

Also worth deciding: attribution. The pill credits the shop and links to them, which is a real incentive for shops to submit good images — and gives the slot a measurable outcome rather than being decoration. Not in the SRS. DECISION

Technical: only frame ① loads eagerly, the rest lazily, or the loop wrecks the LCP < 2.5s target in HP-01. Pause the loop under prefers-reduced-motion, and give it a visible pause control if a cycle is faster than 5s.

04 — Bản đồ khám phá NEW v0.2 — thay hoàn toàn

Correcting v0.1 I described the map as "the project's reason to exist" and drew its stops as shops to go and buy from. Both were wrong. The map shows places worth visiting — somewhere to eat, look at things, spend an afternoon. It is not a directory of Tí Coolture shops, and most featured shops have no physical premises at all. It is an added-value feature that gives a visitor a reason to stay on the site and a reason to come back; it is not the site's purpose.

This matters beyond wording, because it changes what the screens say. A stop is a destination, so the primary action is Mở bản đồ, not "browse this shop's products". Only the minority of stops that happen to be a Tí shop with a physical address get a secondary link into the catalogue. Drawing every stop as a shop card would have promised something the data can't deliver.

The internal analytics value in SRS §8.5 survives intact, and is in fact cleaner this way: ranking which places people click tells you where attention and footfall are concentrated in the city, which is exactly the input for deciding where Tí's own future store should sit. That's a signal about neighbourhoods, not about individual shops.

1
Queue populated from the Sheet, not by hand. Approving creates the shop record directly — this is the step that removes the copy-paste. NEW
2
Preview renders the real shop-page component with the submitted assets, at both breakpoints. It's how you catch a cover that crops badly before a partner sees it live, and it's the reason the preview has to use the actual component rather than a mock. SD-01
4
Three outcomes, not two. "Cần sửa" is the one that matters in practice — most submissions won't be rejected, they'll be missing an image or a story, and a reject/approve binary forces you to either publish something weak or turn a willing partner away. DECISION
3
Automated asset checks, and one scope warning. Flagging dimensions and aspect ratio automatically is cheap and saves the reviewer opening every file — it's also the only practical way to enforce the hero image spec across dozens of partners.

The warning: SRS §1 lists a moderation pipeline (Pending / Approved / Rejected) under V2, gated on legal advice. What's drawn here is that pipeline. The legal concern behind that gating was shop accounts and self-upload, and neither exists here — submissions still arrive by form and a human still decides — so the classification risk stays where D1 put it. But the build cost is real and it is not in the V1 estimate: a Sheets sync, a queue UI, an asset checker, an email template system and a reject/revise state machine. Against a 31/08 launch with one developer, I'd ship V1 with the queue reading from Sheets and the approve action, and leave the templated email as a manual send until September. That keeps the copy-paste saving, which is the actual point, and defers the fiddliest part. §1 DECISION

08 — Còn lại

Cần trả lời trước khi build
  • Hero image spec. Ratio, minimum resolution, safe zone, and whether the shop credit links out. Shops need this brief before they start sending files, so it's the most time-sensitive item here.
  • Price band boundaries in VNĐ. AP-04 says five bands; nobody has written the numbers.
  • Districts and map assets. How many districts, which ones, and who draws or sources each map image. Appendix A question 4 has been open since v1.0 of the SRS.
  • Admin intake scope for V1. Full pipeline, or queue-plus-approve now and templated email in September.
  • Category chip order on mobile — by catalogue volume once entry is done.
Chưa vẽ, có chủ đích
  • Blog (BL-01…06) — conventional layouts, low structural risk, no bearing on the primary conversion.
  • Trang tĩnh — /gioi-thieu, /faq, /dieu-khoan, /bao-mat: single-column prose.
  • /shop index — confirmed in for V1 (IA-2). It's a filtered grid of shop cards and reuses M3's structure; worth drawing only if you want it reviewed separately.
  • Trình sửa map (AD-05) — now needs a district selector. Most expensive admin screen, and the first candidate to cut if the week slips; a manual coordinate field is an acceptable stopgap.
  • 404 / 500 — no structural decisions.
